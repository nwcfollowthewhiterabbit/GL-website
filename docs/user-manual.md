# Green Leaf Pacific Website User Guide

Verified environment: `https://testing.greenleafpacific.com`

This website is not indexed, but requests are saved in Green Leaf's production
ERP. It currently supports quotations without online payment.

## Browse Products

1. Open `/catalog`.
2. Select a department or use search and filters.
3. Open a product to view its SKU, availability and price mode.
4. Add products to your **Quote basket**. You can browse and build a basket before signing in.

Prices are shown in FJD and exclude VAT unless the interface explicitly states
otherwise.

## Create Your Account

1. Open `/account` and select **Create account**.
2. Enter your email and request the verification link. Check your spam folder.
3. Open the link within30 minutes. The newest requested link replaces the old one.
4. Enter your company, contact name, phone and a password of10–128 characters.
5. Complete registration. You are signed in and your basket is kept in this browser.

If you already have an account, sign in or use **Forgot password**. Green Leaf
staff accounts and disabled customer accounts require help from support.

## Request a Quotation

1. Sign in and open **Quote basket**.
2. Review item quantities.
3. Check your account details and enter the contact, phone and delivery location.
4. Select **Request a quote**.
5. Keep the displayed reference for communication with Green Leaf.

The website creates one draft quotation for sales review. It does not confirm an
order, reserve stock or collect payment. Sales confirms availability, final
pricing, VAT and delivery. Invalid/unavailable lines must be corrected before
submission. Retrying the same request opens its existing quotation.

## Customer Account

1. Open `/account`.
2. Enter your verified customer email and password (or existing Green Leaf-issued access).
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Use **Orders**, **Quotations**, and **Invoices** to switch document types.
5. Select **View** to open document lines and the current customer-facing
   status.
6. Select **Sign out** when finished on a shared device.

Use **Load more** to view older records. A new request appears under quotations
as **Under review**. When sales issues a quotation or creates an order in ERP,
refresh your account to see the current status. Orders can be confirmed, on hold,
partially delivered, completed or cancelled. A quotation and a sales order are
different documents; submitting a basket creates the quotation request first.

## Recover Your Password

Select **Forgot password**, enter your account email and open the newest reset
link within30 minutes. Choose a new password, then sign in. Links work once;
the reset signs out previous sessions. Reset cannot restore disabled access.
Green Leaf support can reset, disable or restore an existing account.
Contact `buy@greenleafpacific.com` for access support.

## Online Payment

Online payment is not available in this version. Sales arranges the next steps
after reviewing your quotation. The website does not request card details.

## Report an Issue

Send the page, document reference, time, expected result, actual result and a
screenshot without passwords, cookies or card details to Green Leaf support.

## Website Control Center in ERP

Open **Website Control Center** from ERP to maintain menu sections, homepage
slides, downloadable catalogues, featured products and manufacturer logos.
The Open Website action uses the website configured for that ERP environment.
If it is unavailable, ask the administrator to configure the site's public URL.

Customer access controls are available to System Managers after the private
server connection is configured. These controls use the ERP session; never
paste the website administrator token into a browser or a form. Existing
Website content forms continue to enforce their ERP roles and permissions.
