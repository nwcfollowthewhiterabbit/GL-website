# Green Leaf Pacific Website User Guide

Verified environment: `https://testing.greenleafpacific.com`

This website is not indexed, but requests are saved in Green Leaf's production
ERP. It currently supports quotations without online payment.

## Find a product from the previous catalog

Search the catalog by its full item code. Some products open a sales enquiry page where you can ask for availability and packaging. For a previous listing without an item code, include the catalog reference from the page in your message to sales.

## Browse Products

1. Open `/catalog`.
2. Select **Catalog** to open the department menu on a phone or computer. Choose a department, or select **View all products** to browse and filter the full catalog.
3. Open a product to view its SKU, availability and selling unit.
4. Add products to your **Quote basket**. You can browse and build a basket before signing in.

The **Brands**, **Service** and **Contact** links open their sections of the catalog from any page.

In the **Details** preview, use Tab to move between actions. Press Escape to close it and return to the product.

The catalog, basket and quotation requests under review show units and quantities. Sales confirms pricing when issuing a quotation.

If you follow an older product link, the website opens its current listing when a match exists. For an archived item, send the displayed SKU to the sales team to check availability. For an order placed on the previous website, use the **Orders from the previous website** link on the account page and provide your order number.

## Create Your Account

1. Open `/account` and select **Create an account**.
2. Enter your email and request the verification link. Check your spam folder.
3. Open the link within 30 minutes. Use the most recent verification email; when a new link is issued, the previous link expires.
4. Enter your company, contact name, phone and a password of 10–128 characters.
5. Complete registration. You are signed in and your basket is kept in this browser.

If you already have a website account, sign in or use **Forgot password**.
You can also register using the email of your active ERP account. The website
uses a separate password; registration and password recovery do not change your
ERP password or permissions. Disabled accounts require help from support.

## Request a Quotation

1. Sign in and open **Quote basket**.
2. Review the selling unit next to each item and enter the number of those packs. Two pack sizes of one SKU appear on separate lines. When an item must be ordered in multiples, the catalog shows the multiple and the basket quantity buttons use that step. If a saved basket asks you to choose a current selling unit, open its link and add the desired pack. Its original quantity is retained.
3. Check your account details and enter the contact, phone and delivery location.
4. Select **Request a quote**.
5. Keep the displayed reference for communication with Green Leaf.

The website creates one draft quotation for sales review. It does not confirm an
order, reserve stock or collect payment. Sales confirms availability, final
pricing, VAT and delivery. Invalid/unavailable lines must be corrected before
submission. Retrying the same request opens its existing quotation.

## Customer Account

1. Open `/account`.
2. Enter your verified customer email and password (or existing Green Leaf-issued access).
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Use **Orders**, **Quotations**, and **Invoices** to switch document types.
5. Select **View** to open document lines and the current customer-facing
   status.
6. Select **Sign out** when finished on a shared device. This closes the current browser session. Your other devices stay signed in. Contact and delivery fields are cleared when you sign out or reload. Your product basket stays in this browser.

For purchases made on the former storefront, select **Orders from the previous website** to contact sales.

Use **Load older records** to view older records. A new request appears under quotations
as **Under review**. When sales issues a quotation or creates an order in ERP,
refresh your account to see the current status. Orders can be confirmed, on hold,
partially delivered, completed or cancelled. A quotation and a sales order are
different documents; submitting a basket creates the quotation request first.

## Recover Your Password

Select **Forgot password**, enter your account email and open the newest reset
link within 30 minutes. Choose a new password, then sign in. Links work once;
the reset signs out previous sessions. Reset cannot restore disabled access.
Green Leaf support can reset, disable or restore an existing account.
Contact `buy@greenleafpacific.com` for access support.

## Online Payment

Online payment is not available in this version. Sales arranges the next steps
after reviewing your quotation. The website does not request card details.

## Report an Issue

Send the page, document reference, time, expected result, actual result and a
screenshot without passwords, cookies or card details to Green Leaf support.

## Website Control Center in ERP

Open **Website Control Center** from ERP to maintain menu sections, homepage
slides, downloadable catalogues, featured products and manufacturer logos.
The Open Website action uses the website configured for that ERP environment.
If it is unavailable, ask the administrator to configure the site's public URL.

Customer access controls are available to System Managers after the private
server connection is configured. These controls use the ERP session; never
paste the website administrator token into a browser or a form. Existing
Website content forms continue to enforce their ERP roles and permissions.
